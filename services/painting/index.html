Painting by NAVA INTERIOR

Professional painting for every kind of property in the UAE: apartments, villas, offices, shops and building common areas. We use premium paints from Jotun, National Paints, Dulux and Asian Paints, chosen for the job: washable emulsions for family homes, moisture-resistant paint for bathrooms and kitchens, and heat- and UV-resistant coatings for exterior walls that face the sun all day. Furniture and floors are fully protected, and the work is finished with sharp, clean lines.

  • Ceilings, doors, skirting and trims
  • Feature walls and two-tone designs
  • Full clean-up and final inspection

Our painting process

  1. Inspection & colour advice: We check the walls, note cracks or damp, and help you choose colours and finishes with real paint samples.
  2. Protection: Furniture is moved and wrapped, floors are sheeted, and switches and fittings are masked.
  3. Surface preparation: Cracks and holes are filled, damp patches treated, walls sanded smooth and primed.
  4. Painting: Two finish coats applied by roller and brush, with crisp cut-in lines at ceilings and corners.
  5. Clean-up & handover: Masking removed, furniture back in place, and a walk-through with you to check every wall.

Frequently asked questions

How long does painting take?

A 1-bedroom apartment usually takes 1–2 days, a 2-bedroom 2–3 days, and a villa interior 4–7 days, depending on preparation needed.

Do I need to move my furniture?

No. Our team moves and covers furniture and puts everything back when the job is finished.

Which paint brands do you use?

Jotun, National Paints, Dulux and Asian Paints. We can also use a brand or colour code you already have.

Is the paint safe for children?

Yes. We use low-VOC, low-odour paints, so most families can stay at home during the work.

Can you paint during weekends or at night?

Yes, especially for offices and shops, so your business is not disturbed.
