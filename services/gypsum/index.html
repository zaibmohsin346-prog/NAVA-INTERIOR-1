False ceiling & gypsum works by NAVA INTERIOR

A well-designed ceiling changes the whole room. NAVA INTERIOR designs and installs gypsum false ceilings for villas, apartments, majlis rooms and offices, from clean modern drops with hidden LED strips and linear lights to classic multi-level ceilings with decorative cornices. We build a galvanised metal frame fixed to the slab, level it with a laser, fit moisture-resistant boards where needed, tape and finish every joint and leave it ready for paint, with access panels for AC and plumbing.

What's included

  • Ceiling design and lighting layout
  • Galvanised metal frame
  • Standard, moisture- and fire-rated boards
  • Cove and hidden LED strip troughs
  • Linear and recessed light cut-outs
  • Decorative cornice and mouldings
  • Access panels for AC and plumbing
  • Jointing, sanding and paint-ready finish

How we build your ceiling

  1. Design & layout: We measure the room and plan levels, lighting, AC grilles and access points with you.
  2. Framing: A galvanised metal frame is fixed to the slab and levelled with a laser.
  3. Boarding: Gypsum boards are fixed, with cut-outs for lights, grilles and access panels.
  4. Details & lighting: Cove troughs, cornices and mouldings are fitted and LED strips and lights wired in.
  5. Finishing: Joints are taped, filled and sanded, then primed and painted for a flawless finish.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a false ceiling take?

A typical room takes 2–4 days including finishing; a full apartment about 1–2 weeks.

Will it hide my AC ducts?

Yes. We box in ducts and pipes and add access panels so they can still be serviced.

Can you repair a ceiling damaged by a leak?

Yes. We fix the leak first, then replace and repaint the damaged section to match.

Is gypsum safe in bathrooms?

Yes, using moisture-resistant boards and proper ventilation.
