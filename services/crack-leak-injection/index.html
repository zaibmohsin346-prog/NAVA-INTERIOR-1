Crack & leak injection by NAVA INTERIOR

Water coming through a crack in a wall, slab or basement can often be stopped without breaking anything. We drill injection ports along the crack and inject expanding polyurethane resin or structural epoxy that fills and seals it from the inside.

What's included

  • Crack inspection
  • Injection port drilling
  • PU resin for active leaks
  • Epoxy for structural cracks
  • Port removal and patching
  • Follow-up check
