Wall moulding & panelling by NAVA INTERIOR

Wall moulding adds depth, light and shadow to plain walls and gives any room a refined, high-end finish. NAVA INTERIOR designs and installs classic French-style panel frames with carved corner ornaments, modern slim shadow-line panels, wainscoting, dado rails, skirting and cornices. Every frame is set out with a laser so the panels line up across the whole wall, corners are mitred precisely, joints are filled and sanded, and the finished wall is painted in one seamless colour.

What's included

  • Wall measurement and panel layout design
  • Laser setting-out for perfectly aligned frames
  • PU, MDF or gypsum moulding profiles
  • Carved corner and centre ornaments
  • Wainscoting and dado rails
  • Skirting and ceiling cornice
  • Precise mitred corners
  • Joint filling, sanding and full repaint

How we install wall moulding

  1. Design & layout: We measure the walls and draw the panel layout, balancing frames around doors, windows and sockets.
  2. Setting-out: Frame positions are marked with a laser level so every line is straight and evenly spaced.
  3. Cutting & fixing: Profiles are mitred and fixed with adhesive and a brad nailer, then ornaments are placed.
  4. Filling & sanding: Nail holes and joints are filled and sanded so the moulding looks like one piece with the wall.
  5. Painting & handover: Primer and two coats of paint finish the wall, then we clean up and walk through it with you.

Frequently asked questions

Which material do you use?

Mostly high-density PU (polyurethane) moulding: light, moisture-resistant and easy to paint. MDF and gypsum are available too.

How long does a feature wall take?

Usually 1–2 days including painting; a full room takes 2–4 days.

Can moulding go on any wall?

Yes, on block, concrete or gypsum walls. We check the wall is flat first and skim it if needed.

Can I choose the paint colour?

Yes. Most clients paint the moulding the same colour as the wall; contrast colours also work well.
