AC installation, repair & servicing by NAVA INTERIOR

UAE summers push air conditioning past 45°C for months, so a reliable system is essential. NAVA INTERIOR's HVAC technicians install, repair and maintain every common type of AC: wall-mounted splits, ceiling cassettes, concealed ducted units and chiller-fed fan coils. We size new units correctly for each room, mount them level with neat concealed piping, vacuum and pressure-test every connection, and handle servicing, gas top-ups, duct and grille cleaning and annual maintenance contracts so your system runs cool and efficiently all year.

  • New split, cassette and ducted AC installation
  • Correct sizing and placement advice
  • Copper piping, insulation and drain lines
  • Vacuum and nitrogen pressure testing

  • Refrigerant gas top-up (R410A / R32)

How we install and service your AC

  1. Inspection: We check the room, existing system and electrical supply, or diagnose the fault on the spot.
  2. Recommendation & quote: You get the right unit size or repair option and a clear written quote.
  3. Installation or repair: Units are mounted level, pipes run neatly and insulated, and faults repaired with quality parts.
  4. Testing: We vacuum, pressure-test and check gas, airflow, temperature and drainage.
  5. Clean & handover: The area is cleaned, we explain the controls and give you a service schedule.

Frequently asked questions

How often should my AC be serviced?

Every 3–4 months for units in daily use, with duct cleaning once a year.

My AC is running but not cooling. What is wrong?

Usually low gas, a dirty coil or filter, or a faulty capacitor. We diagnose it on the first visit.

Which brands do you install and repair?

All common brands in the UAE, including Daikin, Carrier, Gree, LG, Samsung, Mitsubishi and O General.

Do you work in chiller-fed buildings?

Yes. We service fan coil units and thermostats in buildings on district cooling such as Empower and Emicool.

Is your work guaranteed?

Yes. Every job comes with a 90-day workmanship warranty. If the same problem comes back, we fix it free.

How do I pay?
