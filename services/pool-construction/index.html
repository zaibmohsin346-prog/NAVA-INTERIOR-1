Swimming pool construction by NAVA INTERIOR

A private pool built around your home and the way your family uses it. NAVA INTERIOR designs and builds reinforced concrete swimming pools for villas, gardens and rooftops across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and Ajman. We handle the design, structural drawings, community and municipality approvals, excavation, steel and concrete, waterproofing, plumbing and filtration, tiling, lighting and the surrounding deck, so you deal with one team from first sketch to first swim.

What's included

  • Site survey and 3D pool design
  • Structural drawings and approvals
  • Excavation and soil removal
  • Reinforced concrete shell
  • Full waterproofing and fill test
  • Pump, filter and plumbing system
  • Mosaic or porcelain tiling
  • LED pool lighting
  • Coping and pool deck
  • Water balancing and handover training

How we build your pool

  1. Design & approvals: We survey the site, design the pool in 3D, prepare structural drawings and obtain community and authority approvals.
  2. Excavation & structure: The area is excavated, steel reinforcement is fixed and the concrete shell is poured and cured.
  3. Waterproofing & plumbing: The shell is waterproofed and fill-tested, and all pipework, skimmers, inlets and drains are installed and pressure-tested.
  4. Tiling & equipment: Mosaic or porcelain tiles, coping, lighting, pump, filter and controls are installed.
  5. Deck & landscaping: The pool deck, paving and surrounding garden are completed.
  6. Commissioning & handover: The pool is filled, the water is balanced and we show you how to run and look after it.

Pool types we build

Skimmer pool

The classic villa pool, with the water level just below the coping and skimmers in the wall.

Overflow pool

Water flows over the edge into a hidden channel, giving a flat, mirror-like surface.

Infinity pool

One edge disappears into the view. Ideal for rooftops and elevated gardens.

Plunge & lap pool

Compact pools for small gardens and narrow plots, or long lanes for swimming.

Jacuzzi & spa

Built-in heated spa with massage jets, on its own or attached to the main pool.

Kids' & shallow pools

Safe shallow areas, sun shelves and steps for children and relaxing.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a pool?

Usually 6–12 weeks after approvals, depending on size, design and site access.

Do I need approval to build a pool?

Yes. Most communities and the municipality require approval. We prepare the drawings and manage the process.

Can you build a pool on a rooftop?

Yes, after a structural engineer confirms the roof can carry the load. We coordinate the assessment.

Can the pool be cooled in summer?

Yes. We can add a pool chiller so the water stays comfortable even in July and August.
