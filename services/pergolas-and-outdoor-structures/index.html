Pergolas & outdoor structures by NAVA INTERIOR

Turn your terrace, rooftop or garden into an outdoor living room you can use all year. NAVA INTERIOR designs and installs pergolas built for the UAE climate: powder-coated aluminium frames that never rust, timber-look composite or real wood slats, and motorised louvred roofs that open for the breeze or close against sun and rain. Every post is anchored to a proper base plate, levelled with a laser, and we can add LED strip lighting, ceiling fans, misting systems and privacy screens.

What's included

  • Site survey and 3D design
  • Powder-coated aluminium frames
  • Timber-look composite or wood slats
  • Motorised louvred roof option
  • Anchored base plates and laser levelling
  • Integrated LED lighting
  • Fans, misting and heaters
  • Side screens and privacy panels

How we build your pergola

  1. Survey & design: We measure the space, check the floor and roof, and show you a 3D design with colour and slat options.
  2. Approval: Where your community requires it, we prepare and submit the drawings.
  3. Fabrication: Frames and slats are cut and powder-coated to size in our workshop.
  4. Installation: Posts are anchored and levelled, beams and slats fixed, and lighting wired in.
  5. Finishing & handover: Joints are sealed, the area is cleaned and we show you how to use any motorised parts.

Frequently asked questions

Will the pergola survive UAE summers?

Yes. We use powder-coated aluminium and UV-stable composite that do not rust, warp or fade in the heat.

Can you install on a rooftop without drilling the waterproofing?

Yes. We use weighted or plinth-mounted bases and seal every fixing so the roof stays watertight.

Can it have lights and fans?

Yes. LED strips, spotlights, ceiling fans and misting systems can all be built in.

Do I need approval?

Many communities require it. We prepare the drawings for you.
