Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about NAVA INTERIOR and our services.

What does NAVA INTERIOR do?

NAVA INTERIOR is an interior fit-out, renovation and maintenance company for villas, apartments and offices in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and Ajman, covering painting, false ceilings, wall moulding, joinery, tiling, waterproofing, pools, pergolas, AC, electrical and plumbing.

Which areas does NAVA INTERIOR cover?

Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and Ajman.

How do I get a quote from NAVA INTERIOR?

Send photos of your project on WhatsApp to +971 56 752 2656 or book a free site inspection. You receive a clear written quote before any work starts.

Is your work guaranteed?

Yes. Every job comes with a 90-day workmanship warranty. If the same problem comes back, we fix it free.

How do I pay?

Pay after the job by card, Apple Pay, bank transfer or cash. Larger projects are paid in stages.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and Ajman, with same-day slots in most areas.

How often should my AC be serviced?

Every 3–4 months for units in daily use, with duct cleaning once a year.

My AC is running but not cooling. What is wrong?

Usually low gas, a dirty coil or filter, or a faulty capacitor. We diagnose it on the first visit.

How long does painting take?

A 1-bedroom apartment usually takes 1–2 days, a 2-bedroom 2–3 days, and a villa interior 4–7 days, depending on preparation needed.

Do I need to move my furniture?

No. Our team moves and covers furniture and puts everything back when the job is finished.

How long does a false ceiling take?

A typical room takes 2–4 days including finishing; a full apartment about 1–2 weeks.

Will it hide my AC ducts?

Yes. We box in ducts and pipes and add access panels so they can still be serviced.

Which material do you use?

Mostly high-density PU (polyurethane) moulding: light, moisture-resistant and easy to paint. MDF and gypsum are available too.

How long does a feature wall take?

Usually 1–2 days including painting; a full room takes 2–4 days.

How do I know where the leak is coming from?

We investigate before quoting, using moisture meters and inspection of the roof, bathrooms and pipes above the damaged area.

How long does waterproofing last?

Depending on the system, 10–15 years for membranes and 5–10 years for exposed coatings when installed correctly.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

Usually 2–3 weeks for a standard bathroom, including waterproofing tests and tile curing. Small refreshes can take less than a week.

Can I use my own tiles and fittings?

Yes. You can supply your own, or choose from our supplier range at trade prices.

Can you install large-format slabs?

Yes. We handle porcelain slabs up to 120 × 280 cm with suction lifters and levelling systems.

Can you tile over existing tiles?

Sometimes, if the old tiles are solid and level. We check on site and advise.

How long does it take to build a pool?

Usually 6–12 weeks after approvals, depending on size, design and site access.

Do I need approval to build a pool?

Yes. Most communities and the municipality require approval. We prepare the drawings and manage the process.

Will the pergola survive UAE summers?

Yes. We use powder-coated aluminium and UV-stable composite that do not rust, warp or fade in the heat.

Can you install on a rooftop without drilling the waterproofing?

Yes. We use weighted or plinth-mounted bases and seal every fixing so the roof stays watertight.

How long does custom joinery take?

Usually 2–3 weeks from approved drawings to installation.
