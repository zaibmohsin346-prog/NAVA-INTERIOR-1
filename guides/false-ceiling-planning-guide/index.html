Gypsum false ceilings: what to plan before you install

A gypsum false ceiling hides ducts and wiring and lets you add cove lighting and spotlights. Most problems come from poor planning, so decide these points before any board goes up.

What is a false ceiling?

A second ceiling built below the concrete slab on a galvanised metal frame, finished with gypsum board. It hides AC ducts, pipes and wiring and creates space for lighting.

How much height does a false ceiling take?

Usually 15 to 30 cm, depending on the ducts and lighting above it. Cove lighting troughs need a little more depth at the edges.

What should I plan before installing a false ceiling?

Plan the positions of lights, AC grilles, smoke detectors and speakers, add access panels for AC and plumbing servicing, and choose moisture-resistant board for bathrooms and kitchens.

Is gypsum suitable for bathrooms?

Yes, if you use moisture-resistant board and the bathroom has a working exhaust fan.

How long does a false ceiling take to install?

A typical room takes 2 to 4 days including taping, sanding and painting. A full apartment takes about 1 to 2 weeks.
