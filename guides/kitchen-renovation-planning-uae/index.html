Kitchen renovation planning guide for UAE homes

A kitchen renovation goes smoothly when the layout, materials and services are decided before work begins. These answers cover the main decisions for kitchens in UAE homes.

How do I plan a kitchen renovation?

Start with how you cook and store food, then measure the space and fix the layout before choosing finishes. Decide on appliances early, because cabinet sizes depend on them. Then plan plumbing, electrical points, lighting and ventilation, choose cabinets and worktops, and confirm building approval before any demolition.

What is the most practical kitchen layout for a UAE apartment?

For narrow apartment kitchens, a straight-line or galley layout usually works well. L-shaped and U-shaped layouts suit wider rooms, and an island needs clear walking space on all sides. Keep the sink, hob and fridge within easy reach of each other and allow worktop space beside the hob.

Which cabinet material suits the UAE climate?

Moisture-resistant board with good edge sealing, or marine plywood for sink cabinets, performs well in humid UAE kitchens. Finishes such as lacquer, acrylic or quality laminate are easy to clean. Use soft-close hardware rated for heavy use, and keep cabinets away from direct sun to prevent fading.

Quartz or granite worktop for a kitchen in Dubai?

Both are durable choices. Quartz is non-porous, consistent in pattern and needs no sealing. Granite is natural stone with unique patterns, handles heat well and needs occasional sealing. Quartz can discolour under strong direct sunlight, so for sunny or outdoor kitchens, granite is often the safer option.

How long does a kitchen renovation take?

A full kitchen renovation usually takes about three to six weeks after approval. Custom cabinets need time for production, and changes to plumbing, gas or electrical points add work on site. A refresh, such as new doors and worktops on existing cabinets, takes much less time.

What affects the cost of a kitchen renovation?

Cost depends on kitchen size, layout changes, cabinet material and finish, worktop type, appliances, plumbing and electrical work, lighting and the amount of demolition. Custom joinery and imported materials add to it. NAVA gives a free written quote after measuring the kitchen and agreeing the scope with you.
