Wall moulding and panelling ideas for apartments and villas

Wall moulding adds depth, light and shadow to plain walls and gives a room a finished, high-end look without major construction.

What is wall moulding?

Decorative profiles fixed to the wall to create panels or frames, usually painted the same colour as the wall. Common styles are classic French panels, modern slim frames and wainscoting.

Which moulding material is best?

High-density polyurethane (PU) is the most popular in the UAE: light, moisture-resistant and easy to paint. MDF and gypsum are also used.

Does wall moulding suit small rooms?

Yes. Paint the walls and moulding the same light colour and use tall vertical frames to make the ceiling feel higher.

How long does a moulding feature wall take?

Usually 1 to 2 days including filling and painting. A full room takes 2 to 4 days.

Can moulding go on any wall?

Yes, on block, concrete or gypsum walls, as long as the wall is flat. Uneven walls are skimmed first.
