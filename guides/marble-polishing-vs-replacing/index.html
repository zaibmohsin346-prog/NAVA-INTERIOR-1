Marble floor dull or scratched? Polishing vs replacing

Marble floors are common in UAE villas and apartments, but sand, dust and daily use dull the surface over time. In most cases, professional polishing restores the floor without replacing a single tile.

Why has my marble floor lost its shine?

Marble loses its shine mainly because fine sand and dust carried in on shoes act like sandpaper, wearing down the surface. Acidic cleaners, spills such as lemon juice or vinegar, and heavy furniture also etch and scratch it. The stone is usually fine underneath; only the top surface is worn.

Can a dull marble floor be polished back to shine?

Yes. Professional marble polishing grinds away the worn top layer with diamond pads in stages, then hones and polishes the surface to the gloss level you choose. Most scratches, etching and dullness can be removed this way. A sealer is often applied afterwards to help resist stains.

When should marble be replaced instead of polished?

Replacement makes sense when tiles are cracked, broken or loose, when stains have soaked deep into the stone, or when the floor has hollow spots from failed adhesive. In those cases, individual tiles can often be replaced and the whole floor polished afterwards so the repair blends in.

How long does marble polishing take?

A typical room can often be polished in one day, while a full villa ground floor may take several days depending on area and condition. Deep scratches and lippage between tiles take longer. The floor can usually be walked on soon after, with furniture moved back once it is dry.

How often should marble floors be polished in the UAE?

Many homes need polishing every one to three years, depending on foot traffic, pets and how much dust comes in. Using door mats, sweeping or dust-mopping often and cleaning with a pH-neutral stone cleaner helps the shine last longer between polishes.

What affects the cost of marble polishing?

The main factors are floor area, how worn or scratched the surface is, the finish you want, whether tiles need grinding to level them, crack or chip repairs, and sealing. Stairs and skirting add time. NAVA gives a free written quote after checking the floor.
