AC maintenance before UAE summer: a simple checklist

UAE summers push air conditioning harder than almost anywhere else. A service in spring prevents most breakdowns in July and August and keeps electricity bills lower.

How often should an AC be serviced in Dubai?

Split units in daily use should be serviced every 3 to 4 months, with a full service before summer. Ducts should be cleaned about once a year, or every 6 months if you have allergies, pets or construction nearby.

What are the signs my AC needs a service?

The unit runs non-stop but the room never feels cold, water drips from the indoor unit, there is a musty smell when it switches on, ice forms on the pipes, or your electricity bill rises without more use.

Why is my AC running but not cooling?

Usually low refrigerant gas from a leak, a dirty coil or filter, a blocked condenser outside, or a failed capacitor. A technician checks pressures and cleans the coils to find the cause.

What does an AC service include?

Filter and coil cleaning, drain line flushing, refrigerant pressure check and gas top-up if needed, electrical and thermostat checks, and cleaning of the outdoor unit.

Is duct cleaning necessary?

Yes for ducted and concealed systems. Dust, mould and bacteria build up inside ducts and blow into the room. Cleaning improves air quality and airflow.
