How to choose an interior fit-out or renovation company in Dubai

Choosing the right contractor matters more than any material you pick. This checklist helps you compare renovation and fit-out companies in Dubai on the points that protect your home and budget.

How do I choose a renovation company in Dubai?

Check that the company holds a valid trade licence for the work, gives a detailed written quote, offers a written warranty, uses its own trained team and can show references or completed projects. Compare at least two or three quotes on the same scope rather than on price alone.

How do I check if a contractor is licensed in Dubai?

Ask for a copy of the company's trade licence and check that the listed activities match your work, such as fit-out, maintenance or specific trades. You can also confirm the licence through official Dubai government channels. Building management usually asks for the licence when you apply for approval.

What should a renovation quote include?

A good quote lists each area and task, the materials and brands, quantities, what is excluded, the timeline, payment stages and warranty terms. Vague one-line quotes make it hard to compare companies and often lead to extra charges later. Ask for anything unclear to be written down before you sign.

Why does an in-house team matter for renovation?

A company with its own team has direct control over quality, scheduling and supervision. When work is passed to several subcontractors, coordination is harder and responsibility for problems can be unclear. Ask who will actually do the work and who supervises the site day to day.

What warranty should a renovation company give?

Ask for a written warranty that states what is covered, such as workmanship, waterproofing or installed fittings, and for how long. Manufacturer warranties on products are separate. Make sure you know how to report a problem and how quickly the company will respond.

What questions should I ask before hiring a contractor?

Ask how long they have worked in the UAE, whether they handle building approvals, who supervises the work, how payments are staged, how changes are priced, and if you can speak to previous clients. NAVA is happy to answer these questions and provides a free written quote.
