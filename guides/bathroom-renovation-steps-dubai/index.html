Bathroom renovation in Dubai: steps, timeline and what affects the cost

A bathroom renovation involves more than new tiles. Plumbing, waterproofing and ventilation all need to be done properly, especially in the UAE where humidity and leaks to the flat below are common problems.

What are the steps of a bathroom renovation?

A typical bathroom renovation follows these steps: site survey and design, building approval, strip-out of old tiles and fixtures, plumbing and electrical changes, waterproofing and a flood test, tiling, then installing the vanity, sanitaryware, shower glass and lighting. Each stage is checked before the next starts, so problems are not hidden behind new tiles.

How long does a bathroom renovation take in Dubai?

A standard bathroom renovation usually takes about two to four weeks once approval is in place. A simple refresh can be faster, while moving drains, changing the layout or waiting for imported tiles and fittings takes longer. Building working hours and approval times also affect the schedule.

What affects the cost of a bathroom renovation?

The main factors are bathroom size, whether the layout or drain positions change, the tiles and sanitaryware you choose, the extent of waterproofing and plumbing work, shower glass, and any concealed cisterns or niches. Access and building rules also play a part. NAVA gives a free written quote after a site visit.

Do I need to waterproof a bathroom during renovation?

Yes. Once old tiles are removed, the floor and the lower part of the shower walls should get a new waterproofing membrane and a flood test before retiling. In Dubai apartments, a failed membrane is a leading cause of leaks into the flat below, so skipping this step is a false saving.

Can I convert a bathtub to a walk-in shower?

Usually, yes. The bathtub is removed, the drain is repositioned or replaced, the floor is sloped and waterproofed, and a shower tray or tiled floor with glass screen is installed. The main check is whether the drain can be placed with enough fall. A site survey confirms this before work starts.

How do I stop mould in a bathroom in the UAE?

Make sure the bathroom has a working exhaust fan vented outside, use mould-resistant paint on the ceiling and walls, and choose quality grout and silicone. High humidity and AC temperature differences make bathrooms prone to condensation, so good ventilation is as important as good finishes.
