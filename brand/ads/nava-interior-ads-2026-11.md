# NAVA INTERIOR – Ad campaigns (Google Search + Facebook/Instagram)

Prepared 8 Nov 2026. **Nothing here is live and nothing has been spent.** Copy it into Google Ads / Meta Ads Manager yourself, set a daily cap you're comfortable with, and create everything **paused** until you've checked it.

All character counts are checked by script (Google headlines ≤ 30, descriptions ≤ 90; Meta headlines ≤ 40).

## Before you start (one-time)

1. In Google Analytics, mark `generate_lead` as a **key event**, then import it into Google Ads as the conversion (Goals → Conversions → Import → Google Analytics 4).
2. Link Google Ads to GA4 (GA Admin → Product links → Google Ads).
3. In Meta, connect the WhatsApp number +971 56 752 2656 to the Facebook page (Page settings → Linked accounts → WhatsApp).
4. Every link below already carries a tracking tag (`?src=google` or `?src=fb`), so WhatsApp messages from ads arrive with “Found you via: …”. Log them in the tracker under *Google search* / *Facebook*.

## Campaigns in this file

1. **Brand – NAVA INTERIOR (all services)** – Anyone looking for a renovation / fit-out company in the UAE
2. **Painting – villas & apartments** – People who need a painting company now (move-out, refresh, villa exterior)
3. **Kitchen & bathroom renovation** – Homeowners planning a kitchen or bathroom upgrade (higher-value jobs)
4. **Waterproofing & leak repair** – Urgent problems: ceiling stains, roof leaks, damp walls (high intent)

Suggested order if your budget is small: start with **4 (Waterproofing)** and **2 (Painting)** on Google – they have the most urgent searches – plus **1 (Brand)** on Meta with the promo video.

## Test plan

1. **Week 1–2 – let it learn.** Don't change ads in the first 5–7 days unless something is clearly broken.
2. **First A/B test (Meta):** promo video vs. best single image in the same ad set. Keep the winner, test the short vs. long primary text next.
3. **First test (Google):** let the RSA rotate; after ~2 weeks check *Asset details* and replace any headline rated “Low”.
4. **Watch weekly:** CTR (Google search ads usually want a few % or more), cost per WhatsApp chat, how many chats become site visits, how many visits become jobs (tracker → *By source*).
5. **Search terms (Google):** every week open *Insights → Search terms* and add anything irrelevant (jobs, materials, other cities) as a negative.
6. **Pause when:** an ad or keyword has spent about the value of one good job without producing a single chat, or chats come in but none convert to site visits (wrong audience).
7. **Scale when:** cost per won job is clearly below what a job earns you – then raise the daily budget by ~20% at a time, not all at once.

## Money

Ads cost real money and are charged to your card. Set a **daily cap** you're comfortable losing while you learn (many small businesses start small and only increase after seeing real enquiries). I haven't quoted platform prices because they change by keyword, season and competition – Google's Keyword Planner and Meta's estimated results will show you the current numbers when you set the budget.
